My name is Ruiheng Wang (王瑞恒 in Chinese). I am a second-year Master's student in [School of Mathematics and Statistics](https://math.xjtu.edu.cn/) at Xi'an Jiaotong University, advised by [Prof. Xiangyu Xu](https://xuxy09.github.io/). My research interests include **Embodied AI**, **Embodied World Models**, **Multimodal Models (LLMs/VLMs)**, **3D Vision** and **Generative Models**.

Before beginning my Master's studies, I received my B.S. degree in Mathematics from Xi'an Jiaotong University as a member of the **First year** program of "**Strengthening Basic Disciplines Plan**" (强基计划 in Chinese). (Ranking **1/63**).

I am always happy to connect with researchers and practitioners who share similar interests. Welcome to contact me for any discussion and cooperation!
